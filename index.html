🎱 2D Pool

Click near the cue ball and drag: the shot fires in the opposite direction of the drag (like pulling the cue stick back), and the power depends on the drag distance.

Pot your balls (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15) and finish with the 8-ball to win. If the cue ball falls in a pocket, it returns to its starting position.

Tip: while any ball is moving, you can't shoot again. The CPU opponent plays automatically on its turn.

Built with HTML5 Canvas and plain JavaScript — elastic collision, friction and restitution physics implemented from scratch.
